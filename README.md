# SnapEyes

Iris art from a phone photo. Marketing page at `/` (React + Vite + Tailwind) and the real studio at `/try`.

## How `/try` works

1. **Capture** (`src/try/TryApp.tsx`): the phone's own camera via `<input capture>`, or a gallery pick of 3-5 shots. There is no in-page live camera: a browser camera stream gives much softer photos than the camera app (no multi-frame processing, no telephoto switch, poor close focus). The photo never leaves the browser at full size: a 1600 px copy goes to analysis, then only the iris square (max 1400 px) is sent on.
2. **`POST /api/analyze`**: Gemini vision returns iris/pupil/glare boxes as JSON, a Daugman-style limbus fit refines the circle, size and sharpness are measured. Verdict: `good` (iris >= 500 px and sharp), `ok`, `weak`, with concrete tips.
3. **`POST /api/deglare`**: disk mask, Real-ESRGAN x4 (ONNX, CPU) when the iris is small, glare mask (relative brightness + vision boxes), and a Gemini image edit that rebuilds only the reflection area, blended back through a feathered mask.
4. **`POST /api/enhance`**: `faithful` = conservative restoration prompt with `thinking_level: high` plus a fidelity guard (low-frequency SSIM against the input; below 0.75 falls back to the faithful upscale). `artistic` = macro re-interpretation for weak photos. With consent the crop, result and metrics are stored to Vercel Blob (`eyes/<session>/…`) as training memory. The page never gets the clean result (`api/_lib/preview.py`): `image` is an 800 px display copy with the preview watermark across the iris, `sealed` the clean 1024 px JPEG encrypted and authenticated with a server-only key (HMAC-SHA256 keystream and tag, 48 h expiry), `sealed_sizes` the same at 768 and 560 px for 3-8 eyes. The order draft (`api/order.py`) opens `sealed` and stores the clean bytes, so `/api/master_eye` gets exactly the enhance output as its preview.
5. **`POST /api/compose`**: takes the sealed irises (opened on the server), PIL composition on the style backgrounds in `api/_assets/bg` with Cinzel/Plus Jakarta typography and a preview watermark, returned at 1024 px. The watermark's words are drawn at the display copy's strength on every iris disc (`iris.WATERMARK_IRIS`), so the artwork and its "Save preview" file carry no clean iris either. Only `sealed` (the clean 1024 px original) opens into an order's preview; the smaller `sealed_sizes` copies open only here.

`GET /api/health` reports what is configured on a deployment.

## Languages and markets

The site speaks English, German, Lithuanian and Hungarian. The language and the market are two separate things, both taken from the link and neither moving the other: `?lang=` picks the language, `?m=` the market (`eu`, `lt`, `hu`, `au`; currency and prices in `api/_lib/markets.py`). Nothing redirects. A page's language is, in this order: `?lang=`, the visitor's earlier choice (localStorage `snapeyes.lang`), the market's own language (`lt` Lithuanian, `hu` Hungarian, set in `markets.py`), the browser's language, English (`src/shared/lang.ts`). The Australian market is English and German only, because the Australian legal texts are.

- The words: `src/landing/copy*.ts`, `src/try/copy*.ts`, `src/order/copy*.ts` (English and German in the main file, `*.lt.ts` and `*.hu.ts` beside it, typed by the English dictionary: a missing key fails `tsc`). The legal texts are `src/legal/docs/*.ts` with `*.lt.ts` and `*.hu.ts`; the editions (EU, Australian, Hungarian with the forint prices) are `src/legal/editions.ts`, and which languages an edition has is `EDITION_LANGS` in `src/shared/legal.ts` and `api/_lib/pay.py`.
- The server: `api/analyze.py` (`TEXT_DE`, `TEXT_LT` in `api/_lib/analyze_lt.py`, `TEXT_HU` in `analyze_hu.py`), `api/_lib/iris.py` (error sentences, `WATERMARK_TEXT`), `api/_lib/pay.py` and `withdraw.py` (emails and receipts, with the Lithuanian and Hungarian texts in `pay_lt.py`, `pay_hu.py`, `withdraw_lt.py`, `withdraw_hu.py`). The order confirmation email carries the legal texts of the order's edition and language from `/legal/order-mail.json`, which the build writes.
- `npm run check:texts` (it also runs before every build) refuses: a Lithuanian or Hungarian dictionary with other keys than the English one, an untranslated English sentence, a dash, a missing sentence for a photo block reason, a legal text missing in a language of its edition, terms that print another currency's prices or do not name every contract language, a consent version in the future or different from the server's, and a withdrawal waiver or statement that is not the same words on the page and on the server. A new function in a copy dictionary needs sample arguments in `scripts/check_texts.mjs`. Hungary is sellable (`"selectable": 1`) only together with its legal edition; `npm run check:prices` enforces that.
- Adding a language means: its dictionaries, its legal texts, `LANGS` in `src/shared/lang.ts` and `api/_lib/pay.py`, its words in the terms' contract-languages sentence in every language, `hreflang` in the five indexable pages and `public/sitemap.xml`, its watermark (measured on every canvas), its analyze sentences, its emails and a new consent version.
- `npm run check:texts` also keeps the two lists honest that nobody reads by hand: a Lithuanian or Hungarian string that is word for word the English or German one, English or German function words in any Lithuanian or Hungarian string (the dictionaries, the legal texts, the built legal pack and the customer sentences in `api/_lib/*_lt.py` and `*_hu.py`), and a consent text that changed under the same consent version (`CONSENT_FINGERPRINTS` in `scripts/check_texts.mjs`: changing a consent text needs a new `CONSENT_VERSION` in `api/_lib/pay.py` and `WITHDRAWAL_CONSENT_VERSION` in `src/shared/legal.ts` together, and the new fingerprint, which the check prints). A word that is also a real Lithuanian or Hungarian word stays off its lists on purpose (`WORDS_EN`, `WORDS_DE`); a word that has to stay English goes into `ENGLISH_OK`.
- Before Lithuanian and Hungarian sales start (nothing here has been read by a lawyer):
  - The one switch for every language and market is ordering itself (the live Stripe keys; `ordering_problem()` in `api/_lib/pay.py`). `"selectable": 0` for `hu` in `api/_lib/markets.py` only hides the forint market.   - Lithuanian: the withdrawal page quotes the model of order 1R-154 with the online-function sentence of Directive (EU) 2023/2673, so check e-tar.lt for an amended 1R-154 (`src/legal/docs/withdrawal.lt.ts`, section `right`).   - Not read by a native speaker: besides the two packs, the lines the integrator wrote (the `eyelid` and `too_small` blocks and sentences, the Hungarian `result.previewFile`/`previewReduced` lines, the market hint and currency footnotes, the forint and euro price paragraphs of the terms, the privacy storage and country-hint paragraphs, the contract-languages sentences, the Lithuanian Stripe sentence about the obligation to pay).   - Withdrawal periods now also run past 15 Mar, 20 Aug and 23 Oct for every order, English, German and Australian ones too (`HOLIDAYS_HU` is in the one holiday table, the latest end anywhere is taken): one working day longer on those days, on the consumer's side. `LEGAL_UPDATED` stays 2026-09-30, the day the previous texts went live: keep the deploy time of this release in your notes.

## Price experiments

Price tests are defined in one place, `api/_lib/experiments.py` (every variant carries its FULL ladder per market; the variant `control` is the standard ladder of `api/_lib/markets.py`; a finished test is kept with `"retired": 1`). Nothing runs until the owner starts it in the admin page "Kainų testai" (`/admin#tests`); the state is `ops/experiments/<key>.json` in the private bucket and every start and stop is in the admin audit log (with the risks the owner accepted). Off, the site behaves exactly as before, and the browser keeps nothing.

- The page asks `GET /api/checkout` without any id, as it always did. Only while a test runs does the answer also name the markets that run one (`exp_markets`). A visitor of such a market (who has not opted out with `?pricetest=off`, the link in the privacy policy) gets a random anonymous id in localStorage (`snapeyes.vid`, 90 days at most, deleted again when no test runs) and asks once more with the id in the header `X-Snapeyes-Visitor` (never in a URL). The server derives the variant from the id (sticky), answers with that variant's ladders and a signed assignment token (HMAC under the ticket secret). `POST /api/checkout` prices the order from the verified token only (`api/_lib/abtest.py`); no valid token means the standard ladder, and a page that sends the price it showed (`shown`) gets `409 price_changed` instead of a different charge.
- The experiment, variant and ladder are recorded in `order.json` (checkout.experiment), the Stripe metadata (`exp`, `exp_var`, `exp_lad`) and `paid.json` (experiment with the ladder that applied); `session_matches` refuses a paid session whose amount is not the price of its own ladder, and the confirmation email names the price list that applied. Because the ladder travels with the session, editing, retiring or deleting a test never makes an open checkout unpayable. Rule of thumb: stop the test, wait 24 hours, then change prices or definitions.
- Anonymous events (kind `exp`: visit, preview, checkout, paid) give the visitors, previews and payment pages per variant; paid orders and revenue come from the order records (live payments, refunded and withdrawn ones apart). The admin page shows the funnel, conversion, revenue per visitor and average order in the test's currency, significance tests (conversion, the affected orders, revenue per visitor; a p-value only with at least 10 paid orders and 100 visitors per arm, and a verdict only once the planned size is reached), the sample size needed, orders that took no part, and a warning when a ladder would sell at a loss (`COSTS` in the same file).
- `npm run check:prices` (and every build) validates every variant ladder (`scripts/check_experiments.mjs`) and that the page's price rule agrees with the server's. Never edit a ladder that has run: add a new experiment key instead.

## Styles

Which styles exist is defined in one place, `api/_lib/styles_registry.py`: the ids, brand names, slugs, eye counts, layouts, the stage ceiling, the price class (`black` or `art`), the gate policy and the sizes a render may work at. How each style is drawn is in `api/_lib/styles_engine.py`, which only Python reads (the public file is imported by the pages as text and ships in their JavaScript). The server asks `api/_lib/catalogue.py` (`known`, `orderable`, `previewable`, `stage_of`, `price_class`, `engine_for`, `tiles_for`, `pick_for`, ...), the pages ask `src/shared/styles.ts`; nobody else keeps a list of style ids, a style name or the rule that makes a style cost the black price.

- The stage in the file is the CEILING (`planned`, `lab`, `preview`, `live`, `retired`); the owner's override in the admin page can only lower it. The six ids of today carry `legacy: 1` and stay `live` until the v3 cutover. The v3 ids are `lab` or `planned`, so no customer can preview or order one, and a style whose engine module is not in the repository is never routed to, whatever its stage says (`catalogue.engine_built`).
- `npm run check:styles` (and every build) refuses a registry that breaks the schema, two ids with one slug, a heart or a pet symbol in an id, a style id written in any other file of `api/`, `src/` or `scripts/` (the allow list is in `scripts/check_styles.mjs`), a copy dictionary keyed by style id that does not match, a layout without a name, and price rules that disagree; it prints the registry hash (12 hex digits, also `catalogue.registry_hash()`). The rules that need the new texts (no number of styles or of eyes in a string, the run-time tokens of the pricing rows) are built and switched off until `WP12_RULES` is set.
- A new style is a registry entry first: add it to both files, run `npm run check:styles`, and raise its stage only in a reviewed change.
- The words for the layouts ("Side by side", "Nebeneinander", "Greta", "Egymás mellett": how the irises of an artwork are arranged) are in one place too, `api/_lib/layout_names.py`, in English, German, Lithuanian and Hungarian for every layout id a style takes. The e-mails read them through `catalogue.layout_name(lang, id)`, the picker and the order page through `layoutName(lang, id)` of `src/shared/layouts.ts`. A new layout id needs its four words there in the same change (the build refuses the registry otherwise, and refuses a word no style takes and any other table that names layouts). The words of the v3 layouts are drafts and wait for a native reading of the Lithuanian and Hungarian ones.

## Style engine: the core, the plates and the bundle

`api/_lib/styles/` is the package the v3 engines are drawn with. It imports nothing heavy (importing the package takes a few milliseconds and loads no engine: a family is imported on first use, `EngineNotBuilt` otherwise), so `enhance`, `checkout` and the webhook can use the profile and the plan without carrying an engine.

- `core.py`, `layouts.py`: the foundation of the design rounds, ported verbatim (the float canvas, splat, blur, the tone map, dither, `Rand` and the seeds, the ring colours and colour class, the disc that is pasted LAST, the solo layouts). `scripts/styles_tests/test_engine_core.py` replays 86 golden cases recorded on the prototype and every one is equal: a port is verbatim when its hashes are. The studio writes nothing on an artwork, so the prototype's tagline, footer, caption and watermark are gone; the process-wide working size became `Iris(max_side)`. Caches are `BoundedCache` (a warm instance renders for hours: no module level dict may grow).
- `text.py`: the customer's own words (names, date, family name) and the draw log that check T7 reads. `unsupported(text)` lists the letters the artwork font cannot draw (read from the font's cmap table, no dependency), so a name is refused before checkout and no empty box reaches a delivered file.
- `selfcheck.py`: the run-time checks of the design brief (T1 the iris is byte for byte the graded disc, T2 pupils clear, T3 visible share, T4 black share, T6, T7, T12), about half a second at 4096 px; `costs.py`: what a design costs in seconds and megabytes, baked from the measured spike (`step_need`, `break_even_factor`, `est_mb`, `assess`). `STYLE_SLOW_CPU` (the factor from the admin action `cpu_probe`) and `STYLE_FUNCTION_MEM_MB` (the project's function memory) are read from the environment.
- `plates.py`, `atlas.py`: the plate library, read only. `api/_lib/plates_registry.py` is BAKED OFFLINE by `scripts/bake_plates_registry.py` from the registries of the design rounds (nothing is fitted, scanned or written at run time; a function's folder is read-only): 221 plates, 169 usable, each with `since` and `until` (the library is append-only: a plate added later never changes the pick of an older `pv`, a retired plate still answers an old one). The 1K files (37 MiB) and the two sprite atlases (6.8 MiB) are in `api/_assets/plates` and `api/_assets/atlas`; the 4K files live in private storage under `plates/v1/<family>/<file>` and are fetched by id, checked against the registry's size and sha256, cached in `/tmp/snapeyes_plates` (written atomically, 256 MiB cap, least recently used out; `STYLE_PLATE_CACHE`, `STYLE_PLATE_CACHE_MB`). A missing or wrong 4K plate raises `PlateUnavailable`: the order is held, no other plate is ever used. `pick`, `pick_n`, `plates` and `Plate.place` are the prototype registry's loader half (the pick is `min(candidates, key=sha256(seed | family | id))`), proved equal on 14 groups of cases. Two JET plates had a legend drawn into the picture by the generator (a pair of checkerboard scale bars; three particle size icons with "(mm)"): `RETOUCH` in the bake script fills those rectangles with black in the 1K file and fits the plate again (the legend had moved the source point of one plate by 1.8 percent of its width); `v3plates` proves every collision plate's fit is the fit of the pixels it ships. No pixel test can find such a legend (every plate holds detached debris), so a family is looked at before it is baked: `python scripts/plate_sheet.py <family> --out sheet.png` (and `--strips 140 --gain 3` for the bottom edge).
- `scripts/upload_plates.py` puts the 4K files into storage (a dry run unless `--yes`; idempotent; never deletes; a stored object that is not the registry's file is reported and left alone). The first release needs `--release1` (Powder Burst's CLOUD, Splash's CROWN, Vortex's SPIRAL: about 370 MiB, to be read against the storage plan before it runs). It reads no key itself: `api/_lib/store.py` takes the service key and the bucket from the environment as everywhere else.
- Where the plates are: `vercel.json` has one entry per function and only `compose`, `master_compose`, `order` and `admin` (the four that render) keep the plates and atlases; the other seven exclude them, and every entry keeps `suites/**` out. `node scripts/bundle_report.mjs` says what each function holds. `npm run check:styles` (and every build) also refuses a plate file that is missing, changed or unlisted, an atlas that is not the registry's, a style shown to customers without its tile images, a rendering function that would pass the 235 MiB tripwire (the files of `api/` plus the recorded size of the Python packages), a `vercel.json` with a glob or the wrong exclusions, and any image of the repository that is byte for byte a reference work or a calibration iris (`scripts/hygiene_denylist.json`, made by `scripts/make_denylist.py`: hashes only).
- `GET /api/health` answers `styles` and `plates_4k` (booleans): the registries agree, and the 4K plates the visible styles fetch are in storage (nothing is asked while no visible style fetches one). The health function carries no plates by design, so whether the FILES are in the bundle of the rendering functions is the build's check and the admin action `plates_status`, which runs in a rendering function.
- The suites use no real eye: `scripts/styles_tests/synth_iris.py` draws procedural irises (fibres, round, slit and bar pupils, three colour classes, an eyelid wedge and a lash fan for the gate). `record_*.py` run the prototype once to record the goldens (they need the scratch tree: `SNAPEYES_SCRATCH_Y3`, `SNAPEYES_SCRATCH_PLATES`); the suites only replay. The suites `v3core` and `v3plates` run them.

### The singles family (`api/_lib/styles/singles`, `api/_lib/styles/matter.py`)

Six single-eye designs: Clean Iris, Powder Burst, Splash, Elements, Radiance and Celestial Gold (the owner-approved variant A, an even corona; the old design is not what ships). They are the design rounds' own code, ported verbatim: `scripts/styles_tests/port_singles.py` makes every file from the scratch prototype by a listed set of edits (imports, plates and atlases read through `styles.plates` and `styles.atlas`, bounded caches, the text drawer of `styles.text`) and `v3single` proves both halves: the committed files are the scratch plus those edits (the step A edits and the one step B set, `STEP_B` in the same tool), and the pictures are the scratch's own, byte for byte, when the old seed is asked for (`singles_cases.py` is the list of cases, `record_goldens_scratch.py` records them on the scratch code into `data/stepA/`, 72 hashes with the plate picks and seeds and 24 hashes of the owner's own calibration eyes: hashes only).

**The seed (WP5B, decision C9 step B, `api/_lib/styles/seeds.py`).** A picture is a function of the iris pixels, of WHICH eye it shows (its `eye_id`, the id of the sealed profile) and of the plan's seed key (registry id of the style, design used, ground, clean flag, layout id, the three options swap, rotate and look, the plates version `pv`), and of nothing else: not the names, the date, the canvas or its size, not the bytes the eye arrives in. The preview, the order's draft and the master of one eye carry the same id, so they seed alike; `resolve` names the seed and the plate or plates the picture will draw from before the render (checkout verifies their 4K files are in storage); every plate pick takes the spec's `pv`, so a plate added to the library later cannot change an older order. What the plan freezes (the liquid of a splash, from the sealed profile) is what the preview and the master draw, and the master checks the seed and the plates it drew against the plan before it stores anything (hold `picture_drift`; a master made from another preview than the plan's is held `eye_changed`). The seed change moved every design but Clean Iris (it draws no matter): the goldens were recorded again on this code (`record_goldens_repo.py`, `data/singles_goldens.json`, `singles_goldens_real.json`) in one commit with the reviewed diff (`diff_goldens.py [--pixels]`), the step A hashes are kept in `data/stepA/` and replayed with `opts seed_mode legacy`, which seeds the old way: byte for byte equal, which is the proof that the seed is the ONLY thing step B changed. `ENGINE_V` is 2. The admin laboratory has the same switch (the Sėkla menu of **Stilių laboratorija**), so a board can be looked at before and after.
- Contract (`api/_lib/styles/__init__.py`): `resolve(spec, profiles)` (the plan without a pixel), `preview(eyes, spec, size, watermark, check)` (a `Preview`: the clean render, the visible discs, the graded frame, the engine's facts, the times and, with `check`, the self checks T1, T6, T7, T12 and T4), `tiles(...)`; `styles.watermarked(preview)` is the one place a preview gets its watermark (an engine never draws it). The family's own entry is `render(design, eye, fmt, size, names, date)`.
- Nothing of the family is visible to customers: every single style is still at the `lab` ceiling. `api/compose.py` hands a one-eye request for a v3 style to the engine only when its effective stage is `preview` or `live`; the admin page sees the others in **Laboratorija > Stilių laboratorija** (the action `styles_lab`: one style on one restored iris, the site's restored sample, a file or the stored 4K eye of a lab test order; no image model, nothing stored; 4096 px comes back as a 1536 px view and a 1280 px window at 100 percent, with the self checks, the colour class, the seed (the customer's, or the old one for a before and after look), the plates picked, the times and the cost table's estimate).
- Run the replay with `bash suites/run_main.sh v3single`; with `SNAPEYES_SCRATCH_Y3` (the `wave-y3` folder of the scratch tree) it also replays the 4096 px pictures of the three plate styles through a local store of their 4K plates and checks the edits, and with `SNAPEYES_CALIB` the real eyes (LOCAL lines, not counted).

## The master plan: how a paid artwork is made (`api/_lib/styles/steps.py`)

Every paid artwork is made by a PLAN, one `art` step for every release-1 style (every design fits one 60 s call at the measured slow factor). The plan, the steps, the claims, the guards and the events are built all the same: they are what the order page, the order detail and the lab show, and a longer function limit collapses everything to single steps with no other change.

- **Records** (`orders/<order>/style/`, small JSON, removed 14 days after `delivery.json` by the daily clean-up): `plan.json` (the style, the design actually used, the layout, the canvas, the eyes' ids, the seed key, the steps with their estimated seconds and megabytes, `plan8`: 8 hex digits of everything that decides the picture and nothing that is only provenance), `done_<step>.json` (written LAST: time, CPU, the increase of the resident size, outputs, inputs, drift), `try_<step>.json` (kills, plate faults, error signatures, `open`), `<step>.lock` (the step's claim, taken over after the lease), `rerun.json` (the admin's rerun count). The artwork of a v3 style is `artwork_<sha256(plan8, rerun)[:16]>.jpg`: an unrelated registry edit never renames an unfinished order's file; a legacy style keeps `master_compose`'s own digest and file.
- **One step through the state machine** (`run_step`): read the plan and the done records; wait for the two process guards (`guard.py`: one memory budget, one heavy render at a time); check the time and memory it needs; claim the step; set `open`; arm the watchdog; render; write the outputs; write the done record; `delivery.json`; clear `open`. A claim that finds `open` still set counts one KILL (the third holds the order); a storage error, a busy answer or a refusal before the claim never counts; a plan that can never fit (`costs.assess`: time over the work budget, memory over the budget, or a fresh invocation that cannot fit it) is a HOLD (`style_step_too_big`), never a busy hop; a second identical exception holds (`style_step_failed`); the second plate fault holds (`plate_unavailable`, the first is a 503 `plate_retry` that is not a `MAX_BUSY` hop); a plan made under another `ENGINE_V` holds (`engine_skew`); a master whose colour class is not the preview's holds (`class_changed`). A hold writes `review.json` and tells the owner once; the order is never made in another style.
- **No room** (`guard.Busy`): a step that finds another heavy render holding the instance's CPU or memory waits up to 2 s, then is answered 503 `room_retry` before it claims anything (it counts nothing). `retry_after` is how long the holder that frees room first still needs by its own estimate (`Busy.eta_s`), at least `ROOM_MIN_S` (5 s) and at most `ROOM_MAX_S` (40 s), `ROOM_DEFAULT_S` (10 s) when it declared none. The server's chain and the order page wait that long before they ask again (one busy hop, but spaced), so a second paid order is not out of its ten busy hops in the few seconds the waits take. `busy_retry` (no time left in this call) is unchanged: a new call has fresh time, so it is asked again at once.
- **The watchdog**: nothing runs after a killed function but the order page (when open), the status nudge and the daily run, so the step arms two relay hops of the server's own chain (`maker.arm_watchdog`) that wait out its claim and then retake it: a killed step is retaken about `duration.watchdog_total` seconds later with no page open. A relay that wakes to a finished order stops.
- **One duration constant** (`api/_lib/duration.py`, `DURATION_S` 60): the work budget (`iris.BUDGET`), every lease (`LEASE_STALE`, `COMPOSE_STALE`, an eye's and a step's claim, the clean-up's lock), `FRESH` and the chain's waits derive from it, and `scripts/check_styles.mjs` item 13 holds every `maxDuration` of `vercel.json` equal to it: raising the limit is one coordinated change, never one number in one file. `PROBE_HOPS` is the longest planned chain (eyes + the plan's steps + the ready email) plus 4, 14 today.
- **Admin**: `order_steps` (the plan, every step with its records, the claims, the capacity at the factor in force, the eyes' ids and sealed gate results, the artwork's record), `rerun_step` (a new file beside the delivered one, never deleting it), `recompose` (the same file when no input changed), `lab_steps` (the master steps of a style on the 4096 px masters stored for a lab test order: no payment, no image model; this is how VE1 reads the real time and memory of a design). The order detail and the laboratory show the step table. `python scripts/order_admin.py status ORDER` prints the plan.
- **Memory**: `peak_mb` in the events and records is the INCREASE of the process's resident size over the step (`VmRSS`, sampled while the step runs), `hwm_mb` the instance's own high-water mark beside it, labelled as that: `ru_maxrss` never falls on a warm instance and would say nothing about one step.
- `ENGINE_V` (`api/_lib/styles/__init__.py`) changes if and only if a golden changes: `scripts/styles_tests/data/engine_v.json` records it with the hashes of the golden files and `v3steps` compares them (`python scripts/styles_tests/test_steps.py --record-engine-v` records the new ones together with the reviewed diff).
- Run the suite with `bash suites/run_main.sh v3steps` (about two minutes: it drives the chain over HTTP with a stub for the image model; a few 4096 px masters are drawn by the real singles engine).

## The Reveal: the photo and the restored iris on one circle (`api/_lib/styles/reveal*.py`, `src/reveal`)

The `/try` hero: left of a hard cut through the pupil the customer's own photo, right of it the restored iris on pure black (`#000000`, no stroke, no glow), and, for one eye, the strip photo, restored iris, artwork. **Server half (WP9):** after the profile `/api/enhance` measures `reveal_params` on the clean restoration and the deglared crop it was made from (the pupil, the registration shift of the photo layer, the restored edge, the colour drift, the eyelid), in about half a second and only with 4 s left, replies the small `reveal` dict (about 150 bytes; `ok: false` when the restored colour is 8 dE00 or more from the photo, or the four half rings of the iris disagree about the shift by more than 0.005 R: the page then shows the strip without the cut and says so, and for a colour drift the colour check's retake advice shows), and builds the display copy from the restoration with its eyelid hidden and its pupil crushed to black, under the usual preview watermark. The sealed copies stay the clean preview. A failing or skipped measurement never costs the preview: no `reveal`, the plain before and after slider stays. `reveal.py`, `reveal_clean.py` and `reveal_wm.py` are made from the scratch prototype by `scripts/styles_tests/port_reveal.py`; the arcs watermark (`reveal_wm.py`) is OFF until the owner signs D14. **Browser half (`src/reveal`):** `revealMath.ts` (the arithmetic, tested against `vectors.json`), `wideFrame.ts` (cuts the photo to the frame in the browser while the page still holds it: kept in memory only, never uploaded, never stored, left out of the copy saved for the way back from Stripe, where the frame is built again from the client crop), `Reveal.tsx`, `RevealStrip.tsx`, `useRevealFrame.ts`, `index.ts` (the surface the landing page's branch uses). There is no wide frame, no card and no context crop on the server (decision C10). Run `bash suites/run_main.sh v3reveal ts`; with `SNAPEYES_SCRATCH_Y3` the suite also checks the port's edits, with `SNAPEYES_CALIB` it replays the ten real calibration eyes (LOCAL lines, not counted).

## Environment variables (Vercel)

- `GEMINI_API_KEY` (required)
- `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (optional, enables the training memory)
- `SNAPEYES_VISION_MODEL` / `SNAPEYES_IMAGE_MODEL` (optional overrides; defaults `gemini-3.8-flash` / `gemini-3.1-flash-image`)
- `STYLE_SLOW_CPU` (optional, 1.0 to 6.0, default 1.6: the slow factor of an instance against a quiet core, from the admin action `cpu_probe`), `STYLE_FUNCTION_MEM_MB` (optional, default 2048: the function memory the style engines budget against) and `STYLE_PLATE_CACHE` / `STYLE_PLATE_CACHE_MB` (optional: the folder and the cap of the 4K plate cache, default `/tmp/snapeyes_plates` and 256)
